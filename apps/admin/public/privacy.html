StudyMate AI — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

Welcome to StudyMate AI ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our users ("you" or "students"). This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide and improve your learning experience:

  • Account Information: Full name, university or personal email address, and securely hashed passwords (using Argon2id).
  • Academic & Study Content: Course notes, flashcards, study schedules, pomodoro session logs, and quiz results created by you.
  • AI Tutor Interactions: Questions and prompts submitted to the AI tutor to generate explanations, summaries, or flashcards.
  • Device & Usage Data: Standard application telemetry and crash logs to maintain app stability and performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for the following educational purposes:

  • To authenticate and manage your student account securely.
  • To synchronize your notes, study schedules, and progress across your devices.
  • To generate AI-powered explanations, quiz questions, and study recommendations.
  • To provide study analytics and consistency heatmaps to track your academic progress.

3. Artificial Intelligence & Third-Party Processors

StudyMate AI integrates advanced LLM models (e.g., Google Gemini) solely to process educational prompts requested by you. We do not sell your personal data or learning materials to third parties. Prompts sent to AI providers are transmitted over encrypted TLS channels and are governed by strict enterprise data protection agreements.

4. Data Storage & Security

We implement industry-standard security safeguards to protect your personal information:

  • All client-server communications use HTTPS with TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Authentication tokens are securely stored on your device using hardware-backed Android Keystore (Expo SecureStore).
  • Database records are isolated with strict user ownership controls.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion (GDPR & CCPA)

We believe you have complete ownership of your academic data:

  • Data Export: You can export a full copy of your data at any time from the app settings.
  • Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated data directly from the app. Upon deletion, all your notes, flashcards, and profile information are permanently purged from our databases.
