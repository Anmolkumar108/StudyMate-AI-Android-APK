THE PROBLEM STATEMENT

The Real-World Academic Crisis in Higher Education

Why traditional study habits and generic productivity apps fail modern college students
1. Severe Information Overload
Cognitive Overwhelm
  • Students balance 5–7 complex courses simultaneously with 100+ pages of dense PDFs and textbooks.
  • Lack of structured synthesis leads to rote memorization and immediate post-exam forgetting.
  • Search engines provide generic, uncurated, or confusing answers.
2. Fragmented App Fatigue
Broken Attention Spans
  • Students switch between 5 separate apps: timers, notes, calendar, flashcards, and ChatGPT.
  • Constant app-switching destroys deep study focus and induces study procrastination.
  • No single platform ties deadlines to daily study goals.
3. The 75% Attendance Trap
Institutional Penalties
  • Universities strictly mandate 75% minimum attendance to sit for final semester examinations.
  • Students lose track of missed lectures until debarred from examinations.
  • Timetable changes and classroom confusion lead to accidental absences.

SECURITY & PRIVACY

Defense-in-Depth Security & Student Privacy

Industry-standard security architectures protecting student academic data
Core Security Standards
Zero Leaks, Zero Compromises
  • Zero Client Secrets: The Android mobile client contains NO API keys, NO database credentials, and NO secret keys. All AI and DB traffic is mediated via backend proxies.
  • Argon2id Password Encryption: Memory-hard hashing resistant to brute-force attacks.
  • Hardware Keystore Token Storage: Short-lived JWT access tokens and 30-day refresh tokens are stored in Android's hardware Keystore (Expo SecureStore).
  • Application-Level Multi-Tenancy: Every query enforces user ownership (WHERE user_id = :current_user_id) preventing cross-tenant IDOR exploits.
  • Verified Password Recovery: 6-digit OTP delivered via HTTPS webhook relay directly into Gmail; strictly manual entry with zero auto-filling.
Student Privacy Guarantee
Play Store Compliant
  • No Data Selling: Student notes, quiz results, and study schedules are never sold, monetized, or shared with third-party advertisers.
  • Privacy Policy Live: Public, verified Privacy Policy accessible on Vercel conforming to Google Play Developer policies.
  • Minimal Permissions: App requests only essential permissions (Internet, Exact Alarms, Notifications) with zero background spyware.
